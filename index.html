Privacy Policy for Learn with Lines

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Learn with Lines application is an educational app designed to help children learn basic English through tracing, drawing, and interactive learning activities.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Learn with Lines application.

Information We Collect

Learn with Lines does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, photographs, precise location, or contact information.

Children's Privacy

Learn with Lines is designed for children and educational use. We take children's privacy seriously.

The application does not knowingly request or collect personally identifiable information from children.

Parents or guardians who believe that personal information has been provided through the application may contact us, and we will take appropriate steps to address the concern.

Permissions

The application only uses device permissions that are necessary for its features to operate.

Learn with Lines does not access sensitive device information such as contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, or SMS unless such functionality is explicitly added in a future version.

Third-Party Services

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

Because the current version of the application does not directly collect personal information, there is no personal information collected by RLS Developer to sell or share.

Data Security

We aim to provide a safe application experience. However, no electronic system or method of data transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the application evolves. Any changes will be posted on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised.

Users and parents are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
